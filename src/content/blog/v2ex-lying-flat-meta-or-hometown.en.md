Read these two V2EX threads together:

1. [Is lying flat the meta?](https://www.v2ex.com/t/1241205) — no job, no marriage, no house, no kids  
2. [Is moving back to a county town workable?](https://www.v2ex.com/t/1241455) — ~¥2M saved, married with one child, rural house, low spending  

The slogan thread fights over definitions. The case thread fights over constraints.

## Split the word first

![How V2ers split the meaning of lying flat](../../assets/inline-v2ex-lying-flat-meta-or-hometown-01.jpg)

The OP listed “four nos.” Replies immediately forked:

| Claim | What it argues |
|------|--------------|
| Low desire is the real meta | Quitting work/marriage/housing/kids is fighting society, not lying flat |
| De-responsibility | Items 2/3/4 are social duties; item 1 (no job) is a survival floor and the loudest fight |
| Lying flat ≠ quitting work | Lower desire, refuse hustle culture, don’t trade health; some joke that double-rest taxpayers are the real “flat” cohort |
| Eligibility | Money-backed rest is lying flat; tight, miserable rest is “lying like a corpse” |
| Walled city | OP already married with a kid—wants flat mode after boarding the train |
| Emigrate | “The meta is to leave”; society will keep making flat mode hard |

![Slogan vs constraints](../../assets/inline-v2ex-lying-flat-meta-or-hometown-diagram.jpg)

## Which of the four nos gets the most heat

**No job** is the only item repeatedly stress-tested on cashflow: “If you don’t work, who pays rent?” Some say a few frugal programmer years back home can last a lifetime; more say gap years / security / ride-hail are fine, but don’t exit society. Someone revived “Sanhe gods”: work one day, lie six—pro max is just having more savings.

**No marriage / no house / no kids** draw less argument. High-frequency line: a child is unlimited liability; every problem returns as a harder copy. Pushback exists too: if you already have a kid and still call parenting a trap, maybe the marriage is PUA-ing you—different income tiers have different low-stress parenting modes.

Experience camp: life shouldn’t be a straight line to death; marriage and kids deliver tastes you only know by living them. Walled-city camp: don’t romanticize the path you didn’t take just because you’re unhappy now.

## ¥2M back in a county town: workable, bill elsewhere

The case thread is more concrete: ~¥2M savings, under ¥100k/year spend in a big city, lower at home; parents healthy with their own savings for ~10 years; spouse supportive; relaxed parenting.

Concern checklist from replies:

| Concern | Point |
|------|------|
| Is money enough? | Most say “enough for your own life”; some cite bond yields or growing vegetables |
| Kids | Biggest split: relaxed parenting vs “once born, flat mode dies / lower niche” |
| School & healthcare | County quality trends down; only works if you accept it |
| Flashing wealth | People will eye the wallet; claim exam-prep / remote work; don’t tell parents the truth |
| Where to buy | Many say skip county apartments; second/third-tier suburbs or first-tier outskirts are stabler |
| Boredom | Total sleep mode isolates; gardening, Xianyu side gigs, a stall as filler |

OP’s own clarification: **flat mode means not punching a clock—not doing nothing.**

![Tradeoffs of county flat mode](../../assets/inline-v2ex-lying-flat-meta-or-hometown-02.jpg)

## One reading of both threads

Slogan posts turn lying flat into resistance theater. Case posts drag it back to a household balance sheet.

Cleanest shared line:

**Low desire and de-responsibility can be strategies. Quitting work with no cashflow usually isn’t lying flat—it’s lying like a corpse. After kids, flat mode stops being a personal toggle and becomes an intergenerational tradeoff—schooling, niche, and the story you tell outsiders arrive as one bill.**
